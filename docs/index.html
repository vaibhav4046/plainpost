PlainPost

Got an official letter you don't fully understand? Paste it here. PlainPost explains what it means and what you need to do, in plain English. When your browser supports it, the explanation runs entirely on your device - nothing you paste is sent anywhere. If not, you choose before anything is sent.

Please read

PlainPost is an explainer, not an adviser. It can misread a letter, and it can miss things. For anything important - money, deadlines, your health - rely on the letter itself and contact the organisation using the details printed on it. If a letter mentions your health, questions go to the clinic or your GP. If it demands money and you are unsure it is real, check the organisation's official website or a number you already trust before paying.

PlainPost has no account, no server of its own and no tracking. On supported browsers (desktop Chrome 139+) the AI runs on your device and the letter never leaves it. On other browsers, cloud explanation is offered only after you agree, letter by letter.
