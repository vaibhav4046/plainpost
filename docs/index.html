AI can misread a letter. Check amounts and deadlines against the original. For money, health or legal decisions, ask the organisation through an independently found official website or a number you already trust. A scam warning is not proof; no warning is not reassurance.

What happens to your letter?

PlainPost does not save your letter or create an account. A compatible desktop Chrome setup with Gemini Nano can explain it on-device. Chrome version alone does not guarantee availability. If the local model cannot finish, cloud processing is offered only with your permission, for that letter.

Cloud processing sends the letter or photo to Google's Gemini API through Firebase AI Logic. Do not send sensitive personal information. Google processes requests under its own terms. Firebase App Check uses reCAPTCHA Enterprise to protect the service; it may contact Google independently of letter processing.
